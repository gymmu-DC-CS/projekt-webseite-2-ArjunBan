What is AI?

🧠

It Thinks

AI software learns patterns from huge amounts of data and uses them to make decisions — like a brain, but digital.

📚

It Learns

AI is trained on text, images and audio. The more it sees, the smarter and more accurate it becomes over time.

⚡

It's Fast

Tasks that take humans hours — writing, coding, summarising — AI can do in seconds with impressive quality.
